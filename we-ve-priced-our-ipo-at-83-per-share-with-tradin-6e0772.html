Anthropic Prices Its IPO at $83 a Share

Why it matters this week: the AI lab behind Claude is leaving private funding behind and stepping onto Nasdaq, with trading set to begin the morning after pricing.

The situation in 60 seconds

Anthropic, the company that builds Claude, has priced its initial public offering at $83 per share. Trading is expected to begin on Nasdaq the following day. Before any of this could happen, the SEC had to clear the company's paperwork: it declared the registration statement for Anthropic's Class A common stock effective on November 18, 2026. The mention of "Class A" stock is a signal that Anthropic has set up more than one class of shares, which is common when founders want to keep extra voting control after going public. In short, a company that has spent years raising money privately from big tech and venture investors is now opening itself up to public market investors and public market scrutiny.

Who wants what

Anthropic
Wants: Capital and public market validation.
Leverage: Frontier Claude models and enterprise/API revenue.
SEC
Wants: Confirm disclosure requirements are met before trading starts.
Leverage: Authority to declare the registration statement effective, or delay it.
Nasdaq
Wants: A high-profile AI listing that boosts the exchange's tech credibility.
Leverage: Choice of listing venue and exchange prestige.
Public investors
Wants: Access to an AI growth story that was previously limited to private and VC money.
Leverage: Capital inflows that shape the opening trade price.
Existing private backers
Wants: A liquidity event and a return on earlier investment.
Leverage: Large pre-IPO ownership stakes.
