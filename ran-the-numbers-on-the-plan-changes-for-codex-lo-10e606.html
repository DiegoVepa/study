Codex Plan Math: Is the $500 Tier Actually Worth It?

Why this matters this week: OpenAI just reshuffled Codex pricing, and the numbers say the pricier plan is the better deal, if you can use it.

The situation in 60 seconds

OpenAI changed the subscription tiers for Codex, its AI coding tool. Theo from t3.gg ran the math on what you actually get back for your money at each tier. He found the $500/month plan hands you roughly $10,500 worth of usage, about a 21x multiplier. The $200/month plan gives roughly $4,300 worth of usage, about 8.6x. So doubling the sticker price more than doubles the usage you get back. Replies compared it to buying the mega roll of toilet paper instead of the regular one, and someone asked the obvious next question: does Anthropic's pricing stack up the same way?
