A Physicist, an AI, and the Standard Model

Why this matters this week: OpenAI just launched a science blog, and its first post is a physicist treating an AI model as a research partner on a decades-old open problem.

The situation in 60 seconds

OpenAI launched a new science blog. The debut post describes physicist Nathaniel Craig working with OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model to produce a candidate way to define the Standard Model of particle physics on a lattice, a discrete computational grid. That specific problem has resisted clean solutions for decades. OpenAI is using the story as an early example of its models acting as genuine collaborators in hard science, not just tools for writing or coding. The result is explicitly labeled a candidate, meaning it is proposed, not yet confirmed by the physics community.
